Wiring overview, revision G

Every connection in the build, as ordered on 2 Oct 2026. Pin-level detail lives in claude/NETLIST.md.

Revision G in one line: the board is the only thing in the box. Both relays, their MOSFET drivers, all four flyback diodes and every fuse are on it. Five field cables enter through the top wall, power plugs in on the right. Nothing is wired inside the box.

1. Where every cable goes

Glands sit straight above their terminals, so every cable runs short and vertical. Terminal positions are the real board X values.

  • Signal and sensor cables
  • Float loop, 5 V
  • Valve cable
  • 24 V in

2. How the valve circuit works

Shown at rest, both coils off. K1 sits on NC, so 24 V drives the close wire. Opening needs both relays on. All of it is copper on the board.

  • 24 V feed
  • Switched path
  • To the valve
  • Float loop and coil supply
  • Ground and diodes
  • Dot = joined. Crossing without a dot = not connected
